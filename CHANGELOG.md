## [0.3.0] - 2026-11-19

### Changed

- **Notices have a title**: every message sheet without buttons shows the sheet title "Notice" above its text, like the other sheets. Aire's own messages use their own title: "Chapter" when long-pressing a chapter in the table of contents, "Font file" when long-pressing a font, "Update" in Aire updates.
- **Statistics lists follow the same rules**: in Time range statistics, the book / week / month cards and the three summary cards use the common card (title 15, bold only when it opens another page, values 13, › on the right); in Today's timeline the book legend rows are 40 high with the book name regular at 13. The file browser list mode shows folder counts at 13 in gray (it used a monospaced 14), like the plain list.
- **Same key / value rows everywhere**: Book information and KOReader's other data pages, Book statistics, Gestures, the Calendar day detail and Recap use one row: 40 high, key 13 in gray, value 13 in black, a thin line below; rows that open another page show their name in bold with › on the right. KOReader's data pages fit as many 40-high rows per page as the screen allows (unless you set your own number), and their text is 13 (it reached 22). The Update chooser (KOReader / Aire) uses the sheet rows.
- **Same cards in every content list**: table of contents, bookmarks, search results, folder shortcuts, KOReader's collections list and the plain file browser use one card: 12 padding, title 15, subtitle and values on the right 13 in gray, top label 11 in bold capitals, icons and check boxes 22. Bold only for what opens another list (folders, collections, shortcuts) and for the current item (the chapter you are in); quotes, search results, files and vocabulary words are regular. The top cards of the table of contents and search, the vocabulary cards, sort rows and the file browser list mode use the same sizes.
- **One header for every full screen.** Main sections show their title with a text button on the right: in Library, Collections and History the current filter and sort ("All · Recent ▾") moved up next to the title, and the counts are now the subtitle; in the file browser the current sort ("Name ▾") moved up too and the path is the subtitle. Subpages (a series or collection, Finished) show "‹ Title" and, when they have a filter or sort, a ≡ button that opens it (the current filter and sort are shown in the subtitle).
- **Aire's ≡ icon** in every screen menu (Book map, Page browser, KOReader's collections list…) instead of KOReader's.
- **Folder shortcuts**: the + on the right is now ≡. Tapping it opens the list options with "Add to folder shortcuts" at the top, as a bottom sheet; long-press adds a shortcut directly.
- **Same spacing in every full screen**: the page dots sit at the same distance from the dock as in a collection, in every screen that has the dock (statistics screens, Gestures, Aire updates, Book information and other KOReader lists); screens without the dock (in the reader: Table of contents, Bookmarks…) leave that same gap down to the bottom of the screen. Sort leaves it between the dots and its buttons, as above the dock, and always starts its list at the common gap below the title (with several pages KOReader centred it for its own footer, so in the Vocabulary builder's "Move to book" the list and the dots were out of place); the Vocabulary builder and the statistics day view leave it below their dots row.
- **Sort** uses Aire's margins: its cards keep the common side margin and the same spacing between cards as the other lists (they had KOReader's), the first one starts at the common gap below the title and the last one leaves the common gap above the dots.
- **Lists to pick from** (Vocabulary builder's "Move to book" and "Filter words from books"): only Close and Apply at the bottom; the first / last page buttons are gone (the dots and swiping change page).
- **Bookmark details** no longer show page dots, and swiping left or right no longer moves to another bookmark (swiping down still closes). The Calendar subtitle uses the common subtitle row; Recap leaves the common gap below its title.

- **Same text sizes in every sheet**: every sheet button (KOReader's included, also those whose label changes) uses font 16, not bold; a label that doesn't fit wraps to two lines at that size instead of shrinking (KOReader shrank each one on its own, so a row could mix sizes). Content text is 15 by default in the text viewer (book description…), the dictionary definition and the text you type, unless you chose another size in KOReader. Check boxes and radio options (search dialogs, open with, sort…) use the row size 15 (KOReader's were bigger), descriptions in pickers and input windows the subtitle size 13, and the field titles of multi-field windows 15. The book description sheet keeps the sheet margin (its text sat closer to the border) and is only as tall as its text needs; the title of the text viewer and the dictionary no longer adds KOReader's own padding above and below it on top of the sheet's.
- **Floating windows are sheets too**: the dictionary (always at the bottom; like every sheet it covers the reader's status bar), messages and notices (information, progress, QR code), footnotes, and the windows with a keyboard (search, rename, notes: right above the keyboard) are bottom sheets with the sheet width, border, corners and padding. Their KOReader title bars (dictionary, text viewer such as the book description) use the common sheet header: title on the left, no line, the menu icon on the right and no X (tap outside or Back to close). Menus anchored to selected text or a button still float next to it. Inside, they use the sheet margins and fonts: the dictionary's title, word, definition and buttons sit at the sheet padding from the border (they had KOReader's smaller margins); the text viewer (book description…) aligns its title, text and buttons with the sheet padding, with the common gaps between them; footnotes get the sheet padding above and below; the progress dialog uses the sheet title and subtitle sizes. Sheets no longer show a "Close" button (text viewer, dictionary, option menus…), since tapping outside or Back closes them; it stays where it is needed (windows that can't be dismissed by tapping outside, and the keyboard windows whose Back and tap outside use it).
- **One header for every sheet**: title in bold 15 and the subtitle lines in gray 13, as in full screens. The book and series sheets show the author and the details at 13 (the author was 16, bigger than the title), the skim sheet's chapter title is 15 (was 17) and the power sheet's Aire version line is 13 (was 12).
- **Long sheets scroll**: choice sheets (Library filter and sort, file browser sort, main view) scroll when they don't fit, like option sheets; in option sheets the buttons stay visible below the rows; very long confirmation texts scroll with Cancel / OK always visible.
- **One way to show what is selected**: the file browser's sort sheet is now the same as the Library's (title, "SORT BY", the current order filled, and Reverse sorting / Mix folders below, filled when on) instead of a menu with the current option as a black button; the main view selector (long-press Home in the dock) fills the current mode instead of adding "(current)" to its name.
- **Same buttons and rows in every sheet**: buttons are 44 high with font 16 and a thin black border (the library filter and sort sheet had 42 and font 14, the update sheets font 13, bookmark details and skim the full-screen pills; power tiles had rounder corners); titles are the same size (updates had a bigger one); option rows, section labels, separators and the gaps between them are the same everywhere.
- **Sheets close the same way**: tapping outside or the Back key close every Aire sheet (power, options, light, library filter and sort, updates, bookmark details); swiping on a sheet no longer closes it nor reaches the screen below. Options sheets with many rows scroll instead of running off the screen.

### Fixed

- **Vocabulary builder**: the first time it was opened after starting KOReader it kept KOReader's margins until it was opened again.

### Internal

- Content lists, step 4: `List.card` can show › on the right (`right = { chevron = true }`); the statistics card lists use it.
- Content lists, step 3: `List.row` in `common/list.lua` (key / value row, `LIST.ROW_H`); `P.row` is now that row, and KeyValuePage rows, the Calendar day detail and Recap use the same sizes.
- Content lists, step 2: `List.card` in `common/list.lua` builds the standard card (title, subtitle, top label, icon or check on the left, value or check on the right, a custom `body`, current/selected state, fixed height or fit to content) with its sizes in `LIST`; the six lists above build their cards with it instead of their own code.
- Content lists, step 1: new `common/list.lua` (`package.loaded.aire_list`) with what the card lists share: walking the rows with the common side margin (`eachItem`), card width and gap (`geometry`), placing the card in its row (`place`) and the tap flash (`onTapFlash`), with the sizes in `LIST` (`common/screen.lua`). Table of contents, bookmarks, search results, folder shortcuts, KOReader's collections list and the plain file browser use it instead of their own copies; every list card (also file browser list mode, sort and vocabulary) uses the common corner radius. Bookmarks drop their own long-press code (the common one already did the same). Visible: the table of contents cards get the common radius and gap.
- Submenus, step 5: `showChoices` builds its content from the width (so `frame` can scroll it); `frame` and `Sheet` accept a `footer` that stays visible below scrolling content. KOReader windows turned into sheets (`toSheet`) keep their own scrolling or pages; when they barely don't fit, only their padding is trimmed (at most the sheet padding), as before.
- Submenus, step 4: `showChoices` in `common/sheet.lua` (a sheet of option groups with the selected one filled) is used by the Library filter and sort, the file browser sort and the main view selector.
- Submenus, step 3: sheet content pieces in `common/sheet.lua` (`title`, `section`, `separator`, `gap`, `button`, `buttons`, `choices`, `row`) with their measurements in `SHEET` (`ROW_H`, `TITLE_FONT`, `ROW_FONT`, `LABEL_FONT`, `ICON`, `TITLE_GAP`, `LABEL_GAP`, `GROUP_GAP`). The options sheet, library filter and sort, updates, light, bookmark details, skim and power use them instead of their own buttons and rows.
- Submenus, step 2: new `common/sheet.lua` (`package.loaded.aire_sheet`). `frame` builds the sheet frame at the bottom (and scrolls content that doesn't fit when given a builder); `Sheet` adds closing (tap outside with the live size, Back), swallowing swipes, and the refresh when shown and closed. Power, options, light, updates, library filter and sort and bookmark details are `Sheet`s; skim and the three-button confirmation use `frame`. Each kept its own copy of all this before.
- Submenus, step 1: the sheet measurements (margin, radius, padding, button height, radius, gap and font, top menu inset) live only in `common/screen.lua` (`SHEET`, `sheetWidth`, `sheetInnerWidth`). `styles/ui/base.lua`, `styles/ui/sheets.lua` and Aire's own sheets (options, power, updates, library filter and sort, bookmark details, skim, book sheet…) read them from there instead of `ButtonDialog.aire_sheet_style` with their own fallback copies. No visible changes.
- Full-screen layout, step 1: new `common/screen.lua` (`package.loaded.aire_screen`) with the shared margins and spacing (`METRICS`) and the only page-dots widget (`Pager`). The Library grid no longer has its own copy (`PageDots` removed), Menu/KeyValuePage/Page lists use the same one, and the side margin hard-coded in `styles/` and `common/pages.lua` comes from `METRICS.SIDE`. No visible changes.
- Full-screen layout, step 2: one status bar, one header and one subtitle row for every full screen (`statusBar`, `header`, `subtitle` in `common/screen.lua`). Aire's own views (`buildTopBar`, `buildViewTitle`, `buildBackHeader`, the file browser title) and KOReader's full screens (`styles/ui/titlebar.lua`) now build them the same way instead of keeping their own copies. No visible changes.
- Full-screen layout, step 3: one screen skeleton and one dock rule. `common/screen.lua` decides when a full screen gets the dock (`fullscreenDock`), how much room it takes (`dockReserve`) and builds the white root with the dock at the bottom (`root`). KOReader's lists, the `Page` screens and Aire's own views (Home, Library, Stats, Calendar, Recap, file browser) use it; the own views share the top part (`Aire:buildScreenTop`). No visible changes.
- Full-screen layout, step 4: one way to give KOReader screens Aire's header. `withTitleBar` in `common/screen.lua` adjusts the title bars a screen creates (full screen, title, subtitle, ‹, menu icon…) and always restores `TitleBar.new`; Book map, Page browser, Image viewer, Calendar, Book information, Sort, Vocabulary builder and full-screen input dialogs use it instead of their own copies. The subtitle is passed to the title bar itself, so the global `TitleBar._aire_pending_subtitle` (and the unused `_aire_pending_default_subtitle`) are gone. No visible changes.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-18

### Added

- **Aire updates from the device**: "Update Aire" in the top menu, right below KOReader's update entry (now called "Update KOReader"), in the file browser and in the reader. Check for updates (long-press checks directly) and Settings: weekly check (only when Wi-Fi is already on; Aire never turns it on) and beta versions (off by default: only stable releases are offered). A bottom sheet shows the new version with its main changes (Skip / Later / Update), and all the changes open in a full Aire screen, formatted; the download shows its progress and, once installed, KOReader can be restarted. If anything fails, the installed version is kept. The previous version is kept as `aire.koplugin.old` until the next start that reaches the file browser, then removed.
- **Power sheet**: Aire's version under KOReader's; the Update button asks whether to update KOReader or Aire, and a card appears when a new Aire version is known.

### Internal

- `scripts/kindle_copy.py` writes the device copy to `../device-ready/aire.koplugin` by default.
- `scripts/l10n.py` also finds KOReader's catalog in a reference copy next to the repository (`../koreader/l10n`).

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-18

First public version (beta).

### Added

- **Home**: top bar, optional large clock, "Currently reading" card (with synopsis and progress) and customizable widgets: Today and streak, Next in series, Previous book, Random book, Quote, Light, Wi-Fi, Battery, Storage, Search, SSH and more. Presets: Reader, Progress, Quick actions, Minimal and Empty.
- **Library, Collections, History and Finished**: cover grids with a bottom dock, filters and sorting. Series and collections are 2×2 mosaics; automatic collections by author and by genre (keyword). Long-press Home switches to KOReader's classic file browser.
- **Fast covers**: covers are kept on disk as small thumbnails already scaled to the grid, and the next page is prepared while the device is idle. Advanced → Clear cover thumbnails if a cover does not update.
- **Stats**: today, streak and finished books, the last 7 days, a reading calendar and a monthly / annual Recap.
- **Long-press sheets** for books and for series / collections: reading status, synopsis, book information, collections, delete and more.
- **KOReader-wide restyling**: rounded dialogs and bottom sheets, dot pagination in every list, cards with a black flash on long-press, SVG checkboxes and radio buttons, capsule progress bars, a cleaner top menu and a reader bottom menu with matching icons.
- **Redesigned screens**: book information (cover header, rating and review cards), table of contents, bookmarks and bookmark details, search results, book map, page browser (bookmarked pages with a ribbon), image viewer, book status, folder shortcuts, collections list, sort screens, the dictionary popup (long-press a highlighted word to delete the highlight; remove words from the vocabulary builder), skim document and note editors.
- **Plugin screens**: Statistics (current book, time range, today's timeline, calendar and reading progress), Gesture overview and Vocabulary builder.
- **Reader status bar**: with "Compact items", the battery shows its percentage.
- **Robust by design**: if Aire's part of a KOReader screen fails, KOReader's own screen is used and a warning is logged; KOReader keeps working.
- **Quiet `crash.log`**: Aire writes a banner with its version on startup; details only with KOReader's verbose logging.
- Startup check for user patches and plugins that overlap with Aire.
- Bundled `aire.*` icons (most based on Lucide) and translations for 44 languages with proper plural forms.
